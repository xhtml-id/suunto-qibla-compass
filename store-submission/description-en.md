# Qibla Compass — Store Listing (English)

## App name
Qibla Compass

## Short description (one line)
Find the Qibla direction offline with your watch's GPS and compass.

## Full description
Qibla Compass shows the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah from wherever you are, using only your watch's GPS and compass. No phone and no internet connection are needed.

Start any sport mode with Qibla Compass enabled. Once GPS has a fix, the watch calculates the Qibla bearing for your exact position and shows an arrow that follows the compass in real time. Turn until the arrow points at the marker at the top of the screen. When you are within ±3° of the Qibla, the arrow turns green, the screen shows "QIBLA ALIGNED" and the watch vibrates once.

**Features**
- Qibla bearing calculated on the watch from your GPS position (great-circle direction to the Kaaba, 21.422487 N, 39.826206 E)
- Live arrow that follows the compass sensor directly
- Clear guidance: "23° TURN RIGHT", "10° TURN LEFT", "QIBLA ALIGNED"
- Red arrow until aligned, green when aligned, with a single vibration
- Two display options, switched with the lower button:
  - Option 1: compass with N/E/S/W that point to true directions
  - Option 2: large arrow only
- The last display option is remembered
- About screen with version and a QR code to the developer page
- Works fully offline
- English and Bahasa Indonesia

**How to use**
1. Go outdoors, away from cars, metal fences and speakers.
2. Choose a sport mode, open its options and enable Qibla Compass under SuuntoPlus, then start the exercise.
3. Wait until the top line changes from "GPS …%" to the Qibla bearing, e.g. "QIBLA 295°".
4. Hold the watch level. If "COMPASS" and "MOVE IN FIGURE 8" appear, move the watch in a figure-8 to calibrate.
5. Turn until the arrow is green and "QIBLA ALIGNED" is shown.

**Accuracy**
The Qibla bearing itself is calculated precisely from your GPS position. The accuracy of the arrow depends on the watch compass, so calibrate it, keep the watch level and stay away from metal and electronics. For your first use at a new place, compare the result with a trusted Qibla reference such as a mosque.
